This page describes exactly what ninhache.fr does with your data. It was
written from the site's source code rather than from a template: what is
described here is what is actually stored.

## What is collected, and when

**If you only read the site, nothing is collected.** No account, no cookie, no
analytics.

The data below only exists from the moment you create a reader account by
signing in with Google, GitHub or Discord.

| Data | Source | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account identifier at the provider | Google, GitHub or Discord | Recognising you on your next sign-in |
| Name returned by the provider | same | Suggesting a starting name, and recognising an account during moderation. Never published |
| Email address | same | Identifying the account reliably. Never published, never used to write to you |
| Profile picture | same | Displayed next to your comments |
| Display name | chosen by you | The only name published under your comments |
| Account creation date | the site | Moderation and account history |
| Your comments and reactions | written by you | Displaying them under the articles |

No password is asked for or stored: authentication is done by the provider you
choose.

## Legal bases

- **Consent** for creating the account and publishing your comments and
  reactions. You give it by signing in, and you withdraw it by deleting your
  account.
- **Legitimate interest** for moderation: keeping the name returned by the
  provider and the email address makes it possible to recognise an account that
  comes back after an exclusion, which a changeable display name does not.

## Cookies

The site sets two cookies, and only two:

- `ninhache_reader`: your reader session, set when you sign in, valid for 30
  days, unreadable by the page's JavaScript. It disappears when you sign out.
- `ninhache_oauth_state`: a short-lived cookie set for the duration of a round
  trip to your provider, used to check that the response received matches the
  request sent. It is cleared immediately afterwards.

Both cookies are strictly necessary for the service you request by signing in.
There is **no analytics, no advertising tracker and no third-party cookie**.
That is why no consent banner is shown to you: the law does not require one for
strictly necessary cookies. The day analytics were added, this page would change
and a banner would appear.

## Who receives your data

Nobody. The data is not sold, rented, or passed to third parties for commercial
purposes.

Two parties see it technically:

- your identity provider (Google, GitHub or Discord), which knows you signed in
  to this site, since it is the one authenticating you;
- OVH, the server's host, whose details are in the
  [legal notice](/legal/mentions-legales), and which hosts the database
  without accessing it.

The data is hosted in France.

## For how long

For as long as your account exists. You delete it, the data goes, with no
waiting period.

Your comments are the exception, and that is a deliberate choice: when you
delete your account they stay online but are **detached from you**. Your name,
your email, your picture and your provider identifier are erased from the
database; the comments then appear signed "Deleted account". This avoids
punching holes in threads where other people replied to you. If you want your
comments gone too, delete them before deleting your account: the
[My account](/compte) page lets you do both.

Database backups are overwritten as they rotate; deleted data may survive there
for a few days at most.

## Your rights, and how to exercise them

Everything happens from the [My account](/compte) page, with no request to file
and no reply to wait for:

- **Access**: the page lists what is stored about you.
- **Portability**: a button downloads all of your data, account, comments and
  reactions, as a JSON file.
- **Rectification**: you change your display name whenever you want.
- **Erasure**: a button deletes your account, permanently and immediately.
- **Withdrawal of consent**: it is the same button.

For anything that page does not cover, write to neo.almeida2706@gmail.com. You may also
lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority, the CNIL in
France.

## Security

Traffic to the site and its API goes over HTTPS. The reader session is a signed
token, unreadable by the page's JavaScript, and separate from the site's
administration token: a reader session can never be used to reach the admin
area.

## Minors

The site is not aimed at people under 15 and does not knowingly collect their
data. If such an account is reported, it is deleted.

## Changes to this policy

This page evolves with the site. Its full history is public, in the source
